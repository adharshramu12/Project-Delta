## If you fork

Pick your own name and logo. The name, creator and academy line Delta uses about itself sit in one file,
`src/delta/identity.py`, so a fork changes them there (see [docs/STYLE_AND_IDENTITY.md](docs/STYLE_AND_IDENTITY.md)).
Keep the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file and the copyright and credit lines, as the licence requires.

## Asking for approval

Open an issue at https://github.com/adharshramu12/Project-Delta/issues saying what you want to do. Approval counts
only when it is given in writing by Adharsh Kumar Bachu.

This policy does not limit any right the AGPL gives you over the code.
