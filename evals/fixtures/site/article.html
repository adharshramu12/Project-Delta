The Kestrel Bridge

The Kestrel Bridge is a fictional suspension bridge used in Delta's eval suite. Construction began in 1998 and the bridge opened to traffic in 2003. Its main span is 1,412 metres long.

The chief engineer was Meera Raghunathan, who later designed the Heron Viaduct. The bridge carries six lanes of traffic and a cycle path on its eastern side.

Every year on 14 March the bridge is closed for a morning so the community can walk across it.
